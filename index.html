The Process

Launching a campaign is easy

Speed and quality are the keys for us when running a campaign — it's a good thing we've got both.

01
Book a call

Apply for a personalised breakdown. We'll outline your exact content goals, build a campaign structure around them, and go through the clipping styles that best suit your content — including which regions to target and how to funnel viewers back to you.

02
Clippers go live

Once we're live, clippers begin posting on designated pages using the agreed styles, with the goal of engagement and funnelling viewers back to you. High-quality clips distributed across TikTok, Instagram, YouTube Shorts, and X — your choice of platforms.

03
Track and scale

Full daily analytics on every clip and every page. We find what's working and push harder on it. This is real distribution. You'll have access to all the data, and once the campaign is over you get the top-performing clips too.

FAQ

Everything you need to know

All the answers before you get started.

What is MGCPM? +
MGCPM is a content distribution agency specialising in clipping — taking your existing video content and distributing it at scale across TikTok, Instagram, YouTube Shorts, and X through a network of 1,000+ elite clippers. We also help with content quality and ideation on your brand pages.
How can this benefit me? +
Turn a few pieces of content per week into thousands of clips per month through mass distribution. Instead of paying per clip, you pay per view — so every pound goes directly toward reach. For brands, this means mass exposure using viral short-form content. This is the new wave of advertising your competitors are already using.
What does it cost? +
We grow with you. Whether it's a $3k test budget or a $300k scale campaign, we'll make every dollar count. Pricing is on a CPM (cost per thousand views) basis — you only pay for performance, not effort.
Do I get to keep the clips? +
Yes. We provide a private folder containing all the best-quality, top-performing clips from your campaign. Use them however you like — ads, reposts, organic content, whatever works for you.
How quickly can we get started? +
New campaigns are typically live within a week — we use that time to build your style guide and brief clippers properly. Any future campaigns can go live within 24 hours.
What is the full process? +
We outline your goals, build a campaign structure, create the editing styles you want clippers to use, then give them access to your content. Every clip is tracked, every page monitored. Daily analytics throughout, and a full debrief at the end.
